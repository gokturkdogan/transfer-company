This Privacy Notice on the Protection and Processing of Personal Data ("Privacy Notice") has been prepared to inform data subjects about the personal data processing activities carried out by Royal Rhein Transfers within the framework of Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), the relevant secondary legislation, and the regulations published by the Personal Data Protection Authority of Türkiye.

This Privacy Notice particularly covers persons who visit the  ⁠royalrheintransfers.com website, inquire about transfer prices, create reservations or transfer requests, benefit from transfer services, or contact Royal Rhein Transfers.

* conducting legal disputes,
* establishing, exercising, and protecting the legal rights of Royal Rhein Transfers,
* fulfilling lawful requests from authorized public institutions and organizations,
* fulfilling obligations arising from applicable legislation.

5. Legal Grounds for Processing Personal Data

Your personal data are processed on the basis of the personal data processing conditions regulated in Article 5 of the KVKK, depending on the data processing activity carried out.

The fundamental data processing activities carried out by Royal Rhein Transfers may be based particularly on;

the processing of personal data belonging to the parties to a contract being necessary, provided that it is directly related to the establishment or performance of a contract,

it being mandatory for the data controller to fulfill its legal obligation,

data processing being necessary for the establishment, exercise, or protection of a right,

and provided that it does not harm the fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject;

data processing being necessary for the legitimate interests of the data controller

may be based on such legal grounds.

For example, the processing of the customer's first name, last name, contact information, transfer route, transfer date, number of passengers, number of pieces of luggage, and, where necessary, flight information is directly connected with the creation and performance of the requested transfer service.

In exceptional data processing activities where explicit consent is required, the explicit consent of the data subject will additionally be obtained.

10. Special Categories of Personal Data

As a rule, special categories of personal data are not requested in the standard reservation process of Royal Rhein Transfers.

For this reason, our customers are advised not to share special categories of personal data that are not necessary for the provision of the transfer service in reservation explanations, contact forms, e-mails, or other free-text fields.

In exceptional cases where the processing of special categories of personal data becomes necessary for the service, such data will be processed only to the extent permitted by applicable legislation and with the necessary security measures applied.

11. Reservations Made on Behalf of Other Passengers

It is possible for a person to create a reservation for themselves together with other travelers or on behalf of a person other than themselves.

If the person creating the reservation transmits personal data belonging to third parties to Royal Rhein Transfers, it is the responsibility of the person making the reservation to ensure that such information has been obtained lawfully and may be shared with Royal Rhein Transfers.

The person making the reservation should, to the extent possible, inform other passengers that their personal data will be transferred to Royal Rhein Transfers.

Personal data belonging to third parties that are not necessary for the provision of the service should not be shared with Royal Rhein Transfers.

Your personal data are not stored indefinitely.

* mandatory retention periods provided for in the relevant legislation,
* limitation periods relating to possible legal disputes,
* periods necessary for the establishment, exercise, or protection of the legal rights of Royal Rhein Transfers

are stored.

If the reasons requiring the processing of personal data cease to exist and there is no other legal ground requiring retention under the legislation, personal data are deleted, destroyed, or anonymized in accordance with the relevant legislation.

16. Rights of the Data Subject under the KVKK

Within the scope of Article 11 of Law No. 6698 KVKK, data subjects whose personal data are processed have the right, within the conditions specified in the Law, to apply to the data controller to;

* learn whether their personal data are being processed,
* request information if their personal data have been processed,
* learn the purpose of processing their personal data and whether they are used in accordance with that purpose,
* know the third parties to whom their personal data are transferred domestically or abroad,
* request correction if their personal data have been processed incompletely or inaccurately,
* request deletion or destruction of their personal data within the conditions provided for in the KVKK,
* request that correction, deletion, or destruction operations be notified to third parties to whom their personal data have been transferred,
* object to a result arising to the detriment of the person as a result of analysis of processed data exclusively through automated systems,
* request compensation for damage suffered due to unlawful processing of personal data
